Using PokerIQ

  1. Select your two hole cards.
  2. Select the community cards visible (0–5).
  3. Choose your table position, number of opponents, simulation count and opponent range.
  4. Calculate to estimate win, tie, loss and equity.
  5. Optionally enter pot and call sizes to see break-even equity and estimated call EV.
  6. Save hands to compare decisions during study sessions.

What the percentages mean

Win: share of simulated deals where your hand finishes ahead of every opponent. Tie: share where at least one opponent finishes level with you for the best result. Equity: your average fraction of the pot, including split pots.

Board study

From the flop onward, PokerIQ labels board texture and counts unseen cards that would complete a straight or flush on the next card. These are raw draw-completion cards, not guaranteed winning outs; opponent holdings can change which cards are truly useful.

Important

The simulator assumes opponents' unknown cards are random. The opponent range presets are heuristic teaching approximations. It does not model betting actions, folds, tells, rake or table dynamics. The output is a mathematical study estimate, not financial advice or a prediction.
