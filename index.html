Using PokerIQ

  1. Select your two hole cards.
  2. Select the community cards visible (0–5).
  3. Choose the number of opponents and simulation count.
  4. Calculate to estimate win, tie, loss and equity.
  5. Optionally enter pot and call sizes to see break-even equity and estimated call EV.
  6. Save hands to compare decisions during study sessions.

What the percentages mean

Win: share of simulated deals where your hand finishes ahead of every opponent. Tie: share where at least one opponent finishes level with you for the best result. Equity: your average fraction of the pot, including split pots.

Important

The simulator assumes opponents' unknown cards are random. It does not model player ranges, betting actions, folds, tells or table dynamics. The output is a mathematical study estimate, not financial advice or a prediction.
